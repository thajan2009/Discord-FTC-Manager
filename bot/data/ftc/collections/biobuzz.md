## BIOBUZZ — scoring elements
- url: https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game
- tags: pollen, nectar, scoring elements, garden, flowers, cells, hive tip
- summary: POLLEN and NECTAR are collected/manipulated and placed in GARDEN, FLOWERS, and CELLS to trigger HIVE TIPS for points.

## BIOBUZZ — match periods
- url: https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game
- tags: auto, autonomous, teleop, driver control, match, period, 30 seconds
- summary: AUTO lasts the first 30 seconds (pre-programmed, no driver input); then TELEOP (driver control). AUTO scoring is assessed at the end of AUTO.

## BIOBUZZ — AUTO scoring options
- url: https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game
- tags: auto scoring, leave, park, loading zone, hive tip, pollen, nectar
- summary: In AUTO, score with HIVE TIPS completed before TELEOP, plus LEAVE (move off the perimeter wall) and PARK (partially within the LOADING ZONE). AUTO-completed HIVE TIPS count toward AUTO score.
